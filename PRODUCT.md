# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students, instructors, and reviewers use AetherOS in a browser to explore and evaluate operating-system and HCI concepts. They need familiar system behaviors, clear feedback, and a simulator that is easy to understand during coursework demonstrations.

## Product Purpose

AetherOS is a technically credible web-based operating-system simulator. It makes windowing, processes, files, permissions, accounts, and core application workflows tangible without requiring a native OS environment.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and quietly technical. The interface should feel purposeful and trustworthy, with the product experience taking precedence over decorative branding.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon cyberpunk styling, generic sci-fi emblems, noisy dashboard chrome, decorative gradients, and close imitation of any single commercial operating system.

## Design Principles

- Make system state legible at a glance.
- Let function lead and keep brand expression restrained.
- Use consistent feedback to make every action feel predictable.
- Prefer calm, flat surfaces and strong hierarchy over decorative effects.
- Preserve a distinct AetherOS identity without borrowing platform-specific marks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep controls keyboard accessible, maintain visible focus states and readable contrast, respect reduced-motion preferences, and never rely on color alone to communicate status.
